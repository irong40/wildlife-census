## Strategic Observations

### Wildlife Survey Drone Contracts: Early Market
- Federal wildlife agencies (FWS, USGS, NPS, BLM) are actively using UAS internally but have NOT yet widely contracted out drone wildlife survey work to commercial operators
- DOI's Office of Aviation Services is the gatekeeper for all DOI drone ops -- their fire UAS solicitation (NAICS 541370, small business set-aside) is the best signal of emerging commercial opportunities
- USDA APHIS Wildlife Services is investigating drone use for feral swine surveys but has not issued formal solicitations yet
- The Martha's Vineyard deer census drone survey (March 2026) demonstrates growing municipal/state demand

### Correctional Facility Drones: Growing Fast
- Oklahoma DOC Skydio pilot program is the proof-of-concept for drone perimeter surveillance
- SAFER SKIES Act expanded counter-drone authority to correctional agencies
- BOP has dedicated but underfunded counter-drone program (~$900K FY2026)
- FEMA C-UAS grants ($500M total) explicitly cover prisons -- FY2027 opens to all states
- SAI opportunity: Provide drone surveillance services TO correctional facilities (pro-drone perimeter patrol), not counter-drone
